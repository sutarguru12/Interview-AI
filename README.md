## Interview AI

An AI-powered web app that helps job seekers prepare for interviews. Upload your resume, paste a job description,
and get a personalized interview report and a tailored resume PDF.

Live Demo:- https://interview-ai-guru-sutar.vercel.app/

The backend is on Render's free tier, so the first load may take 30-60 seconds while the server wakes up.

Features :-
1. Generate an AI interview report from your resume, self-description, and a job description
2. Upload your resume as a file
3. Download a tailored resume as a PDF
4. View all your past reports
5. Sign up and log in with email and password, or with Google
6. Secure login using JWT in HTTP-only cookies
